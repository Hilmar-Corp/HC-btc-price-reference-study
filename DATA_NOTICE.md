# Data Notice

## Scope

This repository contains HilmarCorp research software, methodology, derived evidence and publication figures.

Complete third-party raw market datasets are intentionally excluded.

## Third-party market data

The study uses market information originating from third-party trading venues including:

- Coinbase;
- Bitstamp;
- Kraken.

Those providers retain their respective rights in their data, services, names and trademarks.

The Apache License 2.0 included in this repository does not grant rights to third-party market data.

## Reproduction

Anyone reacquiring market data is responsible for independently complying with applicable:

- API terms;
- market-data terms;
- commercial-use requirements;
- redistribution restrictions;
- rate limits;
- attribution requirements;
- contractual restrictions.

HilmarCorp does not grant a third-party data licence through this repository.

## Derived research evidence

Committed outputs include derived analytical information such as:

- coverage statistics;
- dispersion statistics;
- validation summaries;
- sensitivity tables;
- SHA-256 hashes;
- assurance decisions;
- publication figures.

These outputs are not intended to function as a substitute for underlying exchange datasets.
